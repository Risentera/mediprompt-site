# MediPrompt Landing Context

This context defines the language of the public MediPrompt marketing site. Product terms (voice assistance request, monitoring center, Visit Doctor session, room device registration, …) are owned by `mediprompt/server/CONTEXT.md` and are reused here, not redefined.

## Language

**MediPrompt**:
The canonical product name: nurse-call and clinical communication that works alongside a hospital's SIMRS. It is not itself a SIMRS and is not described as integrated with one. The official logo artwork's wordmark reads "MEDI-PROMPT"; that artwork is used unchanged, and the rule applies to written text only.
_Avoid_: MediPROMPT, MEDI-PROMPT, Mediprompt, SIMRS (as a self-description) — in written text

**SIMRS**:
The hospital's own information management system (EMR, billing, pharmacy), which MediPrompt sits beside.
_Avoid_: MediPrompt module

**Publisher**:
Risentera, the company that publishes the site and receives every pilot demo request.
_Avoid_: Vendor, developer (unqualified)

**Partner**:
Politeknik Artificial Intelligence Budi Mulia Dua (PLAI BMD), credited on the site as Risentera's MediPrompt partner.
_Avoid_: Co-publisher, owner

**Bedside devices**:
The patient-facing room hardware, made of separate registered devices per room: a call button, a microphone, and a teleprompter screen. They connect to the nurse dashboard. The site presents them as being in pilot, without specifications.
_Avoid_: Single bedside unit, bedside teleprompter unit

**Teleprompter screen**:
The bedside device that shows the patient what is happening: that a request was sent, and that a Visit Doctor recording is in progress.
_Avoid_: Teleprompter (for the whole room kit), script page, nurse display

**Published capability**:
A product behaviour the site may claim because it exists in the MediPrompt code. AI urgency is published only as advisory; no compliance, certification, accuracy, or medical-record drafting claim is a published capability.
_Avoid_: Roadmap feature, coming soon

**Pilot demo request**:
The site's single call to action: a visitor contacting Risentera to arrange a demonstration or pilot. The site holds no form and stores nothing; it shows contact links only. Pricing is never published — visitors are invited to ask for a quotation.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, free trial, pricing plan
